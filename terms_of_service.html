In regards to Generative AI

I am not talking about AI to create enemy behaviors; I can still make them perfectly fine, and I am even ok with training a neural network for said behaviors if the need arises. I am talking about the use of generative AI, LLMs, and other tools of the sort to generate things like code, art, and other assets.

In my experience working on several projects, AI-generated code is vastly inferior, and people do not review said code at all, so it's always a nightmare to work with.

  1. Codebases built primarily through automated, unreviewed AI generation prompts (vibe coding) or that I deem unmaintainable will not be patched. They will be entirely rewritten from scratch.
  2. I will not work on projects containing generated assets through generative models (e.g., Stable Diffusion, ChatGPT, Gemini...) be it images, story, sounds, music, voice acting and etc, including placeholders. If your project contains them, work will be paused immediately until removed, or I may remove them myself upon mutual agreement.
